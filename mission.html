Our mission

Realistic security data for every defender.

To give every defender access to realistic security data, so better defensive AI can be built, studied and trusted.

Why it matters

Every organization that protects itself from cyberattacks generates enormous amounts of security data: logs of who logged in, what ran, and what connected to what. Detection systems and analysts sift through it to find the few events that matter.

AI is changing how that work gets done. But building and studying AI for security requires data that reflects real environments, and that data is almost never shared. It is full of sensitive information, and collecting and labeling it is slow and expensive.

We believe synthetic data can close that gap. By simulating realistic organizations, both the everyday activity and the attacks, we can produce data that is useful, shareable and safe by design.

What we value

Realism

Data should capture how real environments behave, including the noise, not an idealized version of them.

Privacy by design

Synthetic data contains no real people or organizations, so it can be shared freely.

Open to research

Progress in security depends on researchers having data they can work with and publish on.

Rigor

Every dataset should come with clear ground truth and be reproducible.
