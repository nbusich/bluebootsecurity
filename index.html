How it works

We start with MDRs and evaluate detection, triage and response.

  1. 1

    Simulate an organization

    Users, workstations, servers and the network between them.

  2. 2

    Generate activity

    Realistic employee behavior alongside emulated attack campaigns (MITRE ATT&CK®).

  3. 3

    Stream telemetry

    Logs flow from our data lake into the SOC's own SIEM and tools, as in production.

  4. 4

    The SOC responds

    It triages, investigates and responds as it would for a real customer.

  5. 5

    Score against ground truth

    Every alert and action is matched to the known attack: what was caught, how fast, and how well it was contained.
