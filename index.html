Security AI is only as good as its data

Defenders increasingly rely on machine learning and AI agents to keep up with attackers. But realistic, high-quality security data is one of the hardest things to come by.

Locked behind privacy

Real logs contain sensitive details about people, systems and networks, so organizations rarely share them.

Scarce public datasets

The open datasets that do exist are small, dated, or too clean to reflect how real environments behave.

Costly to collect

Producing labeled data from real environments takes significant time from engineering, security and legal teams.

What we build

Synthetic data that behaves like the real thing: realistic, fully labeled cybersecurity datasets.

Advance defensive AI

Training and testing data for models and agents that detect and respond to threats.

Supply researchers

Realistic, shareable datasets for academic and industry security research.

Evaluate SOC solutions

A consistent, repeatable basis for understanding how security tools perform.

Our goals

01

Make realistic security data accessible

Give researchers and builders datasets they can actually use and share, without privacy trade-offs.

02

Reflect real environments

Capture the noise, variety and everyday activity of real organizations, not just attacks in isolation.

03

Label everything

Provide ground truth for every event, so data is useful for both training and measurement.

04

Build a common foundation

Give the security community a shared, transparent basis for building and measuring defenses.
